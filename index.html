Your fit rules

Start, then measure

Add your profile to receive a practical first size to try. Brand sizing varies by cut, so compare garment measurements with a piece you already own.

Control the length

Add your height to tune the length and trouser-break guidance.

Keep one relaxed piece

If trousers are wide, keep the top layer short or fitted. If the coat is long, keep the trouser line straighter and the inner layer neat.

Colour without guessing skin tone

Navy, charcoal, tobacco, olive and ecru are reliable together. Test the shirt colour near your face in daylight before committing.
